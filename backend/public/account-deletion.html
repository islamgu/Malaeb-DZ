Account & Data Deletion

Malaeb DZ — Last updated: 26 June 2026

This page explains how to delete your Malaeb DZ account and the personal data associated with it. You can delete your account directly inside the app, or by sending us a request.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Malaeb DZ app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action in the dialog.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account at islamabdelkader03@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

DataDeleted
Account & login (email, name, phone)Yes — permanently deleted
BookingsYes — permanently deleted
FavoritesYes — permanently deleted
Reviews & ratingsYes — permanently deleted

Data retention

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not retain your personal data after deletion, except where a limited retention period is required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

Questions

For anything related to your data, contact us at islamabdelkader03@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
