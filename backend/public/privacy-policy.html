Privacy Policy

Malaeb DZ — Effective date: 26 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Malaeb DZ ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application for discovering and booking sports stadiums. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address (required to create an account), and optionally your name and phone number.
  • Bookings & activity: stadiums you book, your favorites, and any reviews or ratings you submit.
  • Images: photos you choose to upload (for example, when an administrator adds stadium images).

b) Information collected automatically

  • Approximate & precise location: with your permission, we use your device location to show stadiums near you and on the map. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; the App remains usable without it.
  • Device & technical data: basic technical information needed to operate the service (such as authentication tokens and error logs).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To process and display your stadium bookings, favorites, and reviews.
  • To show nearby stadiums and map locations.
  • To send account-related notifications (such as email verification and booking status).
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the App.

5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your account record and associated data (bookings, favorites, and reviews) as described in Section 7. Some records may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and token-based authentication. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can access and update your profile information within the App at any time. You may also delete your account and all associated data:

  • In the App: go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, bookings, favorites, and reviews.
  • By request: email us at the address below and we will process your deletion request.

Full details are available on our Account & Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL, with the effective date updated accordingly.
